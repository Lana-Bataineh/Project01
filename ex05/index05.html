Anthropic discloses fake tip to police among new rogue AI incidents

Oct 9 (Reuters) - An Anthropic artificial intelligence model submitted a false homicide tip to a Philadelphia police website, among a string of incidents the AI company disclosed on Friday detailing Claude models' unsanctioned manipulation of some government websites.

It is the first known instance in which a rogue AI appears to have tried to communicate a bogus tip to authorities, despite instructions not to create accounts or submit anything destructive, while not explicitly barred from submitting forms.


French police arrest three 17-year-olds suspected of running stolen data websites

PARIS, Oct 9 (Reuters) - France's anti-cybercrime unit has closed two websites alleged to be selling stolen data and arrested three 17-year-olds suspected of running them, Paris prosecutor Laure Beccuau said in a statement on Friday.

The Reacher platform was created in March and offered stolen personal data from different sources to the public through different kinds of subscriptions, the statement said.

The service, which was renamed Stryx in June, claimed to store 730 million lines of data, including names, addresses and bank account numbers, and had received 3.1 million requests.

The three male teenagers could face up to 10 years in prison, if convicted.
